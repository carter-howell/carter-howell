# Project Inventory

This is a living inventory for positioning Carter Howell's work across GitHub, portfolio, and resume materials. Keep status labels accurate.

## Featured Engineering Projects

### DIY Wall-E Robot

Status: Active multidisciplinary engineering project.

One of Carter's strongest projects. It combines embedded systems, robotics, servo control, DC motor control, mechanical design, 3D printing, battery power, Bluetooth control, audio, sensors, firmware, and a path toward a consolidated controller PCB.

Known technical elements include:

- ESP32-family controller hardware
- Bluepad32 gamepad support
- PCA9685 servo driver
- approximately ten servos for arms and claws
- JGB37-520 12 V gearmotors around 200 RPM
- motor-driver exploration including L298N, TB6612FNG, and DRV8833
- 3S LiPo battery
- UBEC around 8 A for servos/electronics
- battery voltage monitoring and protection interest
- DFPlayer Mini audio
- IMU options such as BNO055, ICM-20948, or MPU9250
- 3D-printed mechanical components

The custom PCB should usually be treated as a major Wall-E subsystem unless it becomes substantial enough to justify a separate repository or portfolio page.
