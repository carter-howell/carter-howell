## Public Writing Tone

Project descriptions should be technical, specific, and honest.

Prefer:

> Designed an ESP32-based controller that drives ten servos through a PCA9685 and two 12 V gearmotors while monitoring a 3S LiPo battery.

Avoid:

> Built an innovative cutting-edge robot using advanced technology.

Good writing should explain what Carter designed, what he built, how it works, what was difficult, what was measured, and what remains unfinished.
