# Career Goals

Carter's preferred career direction is centered on practical electrical engineering and product development.

Approximate priority order:

1. Consumer electronics
2. Product development
3. Electrical engineering R&D
4. Embedded systems
5. PCB and electronics design
6. Robotics or mechatronic product development
7. Power electronics
8. Power engineering as a stable, high-security fallback path

He is especially interested in roles that span multiple layers of a product:

- circuit design
- PCB layout
- embedded firmware
- sensors
- motors and actuators
- power systems
- communications
- mechanical integration
- prototyping
- testing
- debugging
- product iteration

Power engineering remains an important path because it offers stability, broad demand, strong compensation, and career security. However, Carter would prefer roles closer to consumer electronics, embedded products, robotics, R&D, and product-building when possible.

## Internship Direction

Prioritize internships related to:

- electrical engineering
- hardware engineering
- embedded systems
- electronics design
- product development
- R&D
- PCB design
- power electronics
- controls or robotics
- test engineering

When tailoring resumes or project descriptions, choose project evidence that matches the role. Product-development and embedded roles should emphasize Wall-E, PCB work, embedded firmware, robotics, and hardware/software integration. Power-oriented roles should emphasize circuits, power systems, battery protection, low-voltage cutoff work, PCB design, and relevant coursework or lab work.
